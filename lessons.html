Grip & Setup

Everything in golf starts before you move the club. In this foundational lesson, you'll learn the three main grip styles (overlapping, interlocking, baseball) and understand why the neutral grip produces the most consistent results. We cover correct hand placement, grip pressure (light enough to feel the clubhead), and the checklist for a balanced, athletic address position.

  • Vardon (overlapping) vs interlocking grip comparison
  • V-shaped hand position and wrist alignment
  • Stance width relative to club selection
  • Ball position: irons, fairway woods, driver
  • Spine tilt, knee flex, and weight distribution

Basic Swing Mechanics

The golf swing is a rotational movement, not a lift. This lesson breaks the full swing into its key positions: takeaway, halfway back, top of backswing, transition, impact, and follow-through. Using slow-motion video analysis, we identify early faults and build muscle memory through structured drills.

  • One-piece takeaway and club path in the backswing
  • Hip turn vs shoulder turn — feel the difference
  • The magic of the "lag" and late release
  • Square impact: face angle and swing path basics
  • Balanced finish and weight transfer drill
⏱ 60 min Book Now
🕳️

Putting Fundamentals

Roughly 40% of your shots happen on the green — so putting is the fastest way to lower your score. This lesson covers the pendulum stroke, consistent eye position over the ball, reading green slopes, and distance control through tempo rather than force. You'll leave with a 3-step pre-putt routine you can use every round.

  • Putter grip styles: conventional, cross-hand, claw
  • Eye-over-ball setup for accurate alignment
  • Pendulum stroke and shoulder-driven motion
  • Green reading: slope, grain, and speed
  • Lag putting — eliminating three-putts

Course Management

Play smarter, score lower — without improving your swing. Course management is the art of decision-making on the course: when to attack, when to lay up, how to use the whole fairway, and how to minimise bogeys into doubles. We'll walk through real hole scenarios with strategic analysis.

  • Playing to your yardage gaps, not the pin
  • Tee-shot strategy: driver vs fairway wood
  • Identifying safe miss zones on every approach
  • Par-5 planning: eagle, birdie or smart par
  • Danger recognition: water, OB, and hidden hazards
⏱ 90 min Book Now

Iron Play

Consistent iron play separates mid-handicappers from low-handicappers. This lesson focuses on ball-first contact (the divot after the ball), controlling trajectory and spin, and gapping your set so you never have to "guide" a shot. Includes full bag fitting review and real on-course application.

  • Descending blow concept and hitting the sweet spot
  • Shaft lean at impact for compression
  • Controlling carry distance — full, three-quarter, half shots
  • Trajectory control: high vs punch shot
  • Practice routine: the 9-shot drill
⏱ 60 min Book Now
🏖️

Bunker Shots

Sand saves are one of the most satisfying shots in golf once you have the technique. Learn the classic open-face splash where the club enters the sand 2 inches behind the ball, the correct follow-through, and how to adjust for wet sand, fried-egg lies, and steep-faced bunkers.

  • Open clubface setup and wide stance
  • Entry point: the "dollar bill" drill
  • Acceleration through impact — no decelerating
  • Adjusting for soft vs firm sand
  • Fairway bunker technique: clean contact first

Shot Shaping

Intentional ball flight is the hallmark of a skilled golfer. This advanced lesson covers the D-plane model of ball flight: how the combination of face angle and swing path produces draws, fades, high cuts, low punches, and stingers. We use TrackMan data to measure your existing patterns and build on them.

  • D-Plane theory: face angle starts the ball, path curves it
  • Intentional draw: in-to-out path with slightly closed face
  • Intentional fade: out-to-in path with slightly open face
  • Power stinger: low spinner under the wind
  • Practice pattern: the 3×3 shot matrix drill
⏱ 90 min Book Now
💨

Wind Play

Wind separates great ball-strikers from great scorers. Understand how into-wind shots require a lower, more compact swing, how to "hold off" a draw into the right-to-left wind, and when to use the wind to set up an aggressive pin attack. Includes downwind power play techniques.

  • Into the wind: club up, grip down, abbreviated swing
  • Crosswind: allow for curve vs fight the wind
  • Downwind: using higher tee, more loft for carry
  • Wet and cold conditions: distance adjustment tables
  • Reading wind from treetops, flag, and feel
⏱ 90 min Book Now

Mental Game & Peak Performance

At the advanced level, the biggest performance gap is mental. This session draws on sport psychology principles to develop a pre-shot routine that eliminates mechanical thinking, builds a "process over outcome" mindset, and teaches you how to stay present and bounce back from poor shots. Used by tour players worldwide.

  • Building a consistent pre-shot routine (5 seconds to commit)
  • Target focus vs swing-thought focus on the course
  • Breathing and body reset after a bad shot
  • Competitive round preparation and game-day routines
  • Developing a personal scoring goal and strategy
