Why Irons — Not Your Driver

Every golfer wants to hit it farther. But that's not what lowers your score.

📊

Where Strokes Come From

In a typical round you use your irons on nearly every approach shot, every par-3, and most layups. Your driver? Maybe 14 swings. Fix the club you actually use the most.

🎯

Irons Set Up Everything

A solid iron shot lands you on or near the green in position to score. A bad one creates recovery shots, penalty strokes, and extra putts. Iron consistency is the whole game.

💡

The Foundation

Once you build a consistent iron swing, the driver comes naturally — it's the same motion. But you can't work backwards. Get your irons right first, always.

The 7 Lessons

Built in order. Each one unlocks the next.

01

Grip That Controls the Clubface

The most overlooked fundamental. Get this wrong and nothing else can save you. Learn the exact hand position that gives you control without thinking about it on every shot.

Foundation
02

Setup & Distance (The Contact Foundation)

How you stand to the ball determines contact before you even swing. Keep the ball close, bend from the hips, shoulder slightly back. Small adjustments, huge consistency gains.

Foundation
03

The Turn: The Move That Controls Your Swing

This is the big one. Most amateur misses trace back here. When your arms outrace your body, the face stays open. When you make a full body turn, everything falls into place naturally.

Key Concept
04

Backswing Simplified: Turn, Then Set

The backswing isn't complicated once you know the sequence: turn first, then set your wrists. Slow it down. At the top, your lead wrist should feel flat — like carrying a tray.

Technique
05

Smooth Tempo: Stop Swinging Too Hard

Swinging harder does not make the ball go farther — it makes it go sideways. Learn to feel the difference between effort and speed. A smooth swing beats max effort every time.

Technique
06

Natural Release: Right Hand Back, Left Hand Through

A good release is body-driven. When your body stops turning, your hands flip to compensate — and that's where bad shots come from. Learn to let the release happen naturally.

Technique
07

The Iron Progression System: Same Swing, Different Club

You don't need a different swing for each iron. The same fundamentals apply from a 4-iron to a pitching wedge. Apply everything you've learned across your full iron set.
