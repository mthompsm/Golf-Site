Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my first lesson?

Just wear comfortable athletic clothing and bring any clubs you own. If you're a complete beginner with no clubs, we provide loaner equipment at no extra charge. Golf shoes are recommended but not required — trainers with good grip are fine.

How many lessons will I need before I can play a full round?

Most complete beginners are ready to play a relaxed 9-hole round after 3–4 lessons. You won't be shooting par, but you'll have the fundamentals needed to enjoy the course without frustration. Everyone progresses differently — we'll guide you honestly.

Do you offer group lessons?

Yes. Groups of 2–4 players receive a 20% discount per person on all lesson packages. This is popular among friends and couples learning together. Corporate team-building packages are also available — contact us for details.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations (under 12 hours) are charged at 50% of the session fee. No-shows are charged the full fee. We understand emergencies happen — just call or message us as soon as possible.

Do you teach juniors?

Absolutely. Coach Mike is passionate about junior golf and has helped many young players develop lifelong love of the game. Junior rates are available for students under 17. We also offer parent-and-child lesson packages — a fantastic way to share the sport as a family.

Is the monthly unlimited plan truly unlimited?

Yes — subject to schedule availability. Most unlimited members book 2–4 sessions per week. The plan renews monthly and can be cancelled at any time with no penalty. It's ideal for students who are serious about rapid improvement.
