## Pre-Shot Checklist (The 4-Point Reset)

1. Grip correct?
2. Ball close — not reaching?
3. Shoulder slightly back?
4. Committed to a full turn?

## Why Irons First

- Driver: ~14 swings per round
- Irons: ~20+ swings per round
- Putter: ~30–36 swings per round
- Irons are where scoring opportunities are created
- Iron consistency unlocks the whole game
- Same iron swing carries over to driver naturally
