# Marketing Plan — Stop Overthinking Your Irons

## Core Message

> "Most golf instruction is designed to keep you coming back. This isn't that.
> Simple, natural advice from a real golfer — not a PGA pro with a business
> model built on your confusion. No overwhelming swing thoughts. No ongoing
> lessons. Just the fundamentals that actually work."

---

## The Hook That Works

> "I played as a high handicapper for 20 years. Here's what nobody told me."

Use this everywhere — TikTok bio, ad copy, email subject lines, Reddit posts.

---

## Three Pain Points You Address

1. **Complexity** — too many swing thoughts, too much to remember
2. **Cost** — lessons are expensive and never-ending
3. **Trust** — is my instructor really giving me everything?

---

## Platform Strategy

### Phase 1 — Organic (Free) First

#### TikTok (Primary Platform)
- Short form fits the content perfectly — one tip, 30–60 seconds
- Golf content performs very well — passionate engaged community
- New accounts CAN go viral — discovery is built into the algorithm
- Your face, your voice, phone camera — authentic beats polished
- Post 2–3x per week minimum for first 3 months

**Account setup:**
- Name ideas: `@stopoverthinkinggolf`, `@simplygolf`, `@realgolftips`
- Bio: "High handicapper who finally figured it out. Simple golf tips. Link below."
- Website link in bio from day one

**First 10 Video Ideas:**
1. "I took lessons for years and nobody told me this"
2. "The one thing that finally fixed my slice"
3. "Why your irons are so inconsistent — it's not what you think"
4. "Stop swinging so hard — here's what actually adds distance"
5. "The grip mistake 90% of high handicappers make"
6. "Why irons matter more than your driver"
7. "The turn — the one move that fixes everything"
8. "I hit better with my eyes closed — here's why"
9. "What $150/hour lessons never told me"
10. "The 4 things I check before every single shot"

**Content formula:**
- Hook in first 2 seconds (the problem/result)
- Quick tip or story (the content)
- Soft call to action ("Full guide in my bio")
- Never hard sell in the video itself

#### Reddit (Secondary)
- r/golf — 500k+ members, lots of high handicappers
- r/golfswing — people actively seeking swing advice
- Strategy: Be genuinely helpful, answer questions, share tips
- Do NOT spam your product link — build credibility first
- After 10+ helpful posts, a soft mention is natural and accepted

#### Facebook Golf Groups
- Search "golf tips", "high handicap golf", "beginner golf"
- Same strategy as Reddit — help first, sell never (directly)

---

### Phase 2 — Paid Ads (After Organic Works)

#### TikTok Ads
- Only start after organic content is getting views
- Use your best-performing organic video as the first ad
- Target: golf interest, ages 35–65, US/Canada/UK/Australia
- Budget: start at $10–20/day, scale what works
- Goal: send traffic to website, not Gumroad directly

#### Google Ads
- Only after you have testimonials and proven conversions
- Target keywords:
  - "how to stop slicing irons"
  - "high handicap golf tips"
  - "simple golf swing for beginners"
  - "golf iron consistency"
  - "golf instruction for beginners"
- Budget: start small, track cost per sale

---

### Phase 3 — SEO (Free, Long Term)

**Google Search Console**
- Submit site at search.google.com/search-console
- Free — gets you indexed immediately
- Takes 5 minutes

**Basic SEO already built into site:**
- Page titles include keywords
- Meta descriptions written
- Semantic HTML structure

**Future content ideas for SEO:**
- Blog posts on each lesson topic
- "How to stop slicing your irons" article
- "Best practice drills for high handicappers"

---

## Pricing Psychology

- $9.99 = impulse buy, no hesitation
- Less than one bucket of balls
- Less than 10% of one hour lesson
- The low price HELPS conversion — removes doubt about risk

---

## When You Have Testimonials

Add them to the website immediately. Even one or two real results ("dropped 5 strokes in a month") changes conversion dramatically. Collect them from:
- Friends and club members you share it with first
- Early Gumroad buyers — follow up by email

---

## The Series Play (Long Term)

Each product sells the next:
- Irons buyer → offer Driver guide when ready
- Email list from Gumroad = warm audience for every future product
- Goal: build a list of buyers who trust you
