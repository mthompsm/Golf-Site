# Golf Content — Raw Lesson Notes & Key Concepts

## The Core Philosophy

Golf is simpler than everyone makes it. A few fundamentals done right beat
a thousand tips every time. Stop overthinking. Make it natural.

---

## The Single Most Important Concept

**Make the turn — then let the hands follow.**

Everything else flows from this. Most amateur misses trace back to not making
a full body turn. When arms outrace the body, the face stays open = slice.

Backup swing cue: "Turn → set → swing smooth."

---

## Key Themes (Recurring Across All Lessons)

1. **The Turn** — most important, most common failure point
2. **Grip** — foundation everything sits on
3. **Ball position** — keep close, don't reach
4. **Slow backswing** — comes up constantly
5. **Same swing for all irons** — don't overthink club changes

## Key Feels & Drills That Work

| Drill/Feel | What It Teaches |
|------------|-----------------|
| Eyes closed swings | Feel vs. visual — improves sequencing |
| Underhand throw motion | Proper path and release |
| "Serving a tray" | Correct wrist position at top |
| "Playing card in glove" | Stable wrist, don't bend |
| Strike spray / face tape | Instant contact feedback |
| Slow backswing | Prevents arms from outracing body |
| Grab object + twist | Feeling the release/twisting motion |

## Common Miss Patterns

| Miss | Cause |
|------|-------|
| Slice / push | Arms outracing body, face open |
| Fat / thin | Reaching for ball, poor posture |
| Inconsistent contact | Ball too far away, changing setup |
| Loss of distance | Swinging too hard, no turn |
| Open face | Not enough turn, weak grip |

## The Full Swing Playbook

### Setup
- Slight right shoulder back
- Slight right foot back
- Bend from hips, not reaching
- Ball close to body
- Club pointed toward left hip

### Grip
- Lead hand V → left shoulder
- Trail hand V → right shoulder
- Slightly strong (turned over)
- Thumb-to-thumb
- Same for all clubs

### Backswing
- Slow, controlled takeaway
- Turn FIRST, then set wrists
- At top: wrist flat ("tray position")
- Goes UP, not around
- Avoid lifting, avoid head movement

### Downswing
- Body leads, arms follow
- Keep shoulder back through impact
- Don't hack at the ball
- Swing smooth, not max effort

### Release
- Slight hand turnover through impact
- Body-driven — not hands flipping
- If feels like over-rotating, body stopped turning

### Lower Body
- Don't collapse right knee inward
- Hips must rotate (not slide, not stall)
- Maintain structure through turn

## Pre-Shot Checklist

1. Grip correct?
2. Ball close — not reaching?
3. Shoulder slightly back?
4. Full turn commitment?
